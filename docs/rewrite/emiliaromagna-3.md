# Emilia-Romagna 3 — Parmigiano, prosciutto and balsamic. Outline and ledger (2026-09-14)

Status: batch region, first batch. Title stays. Summary now: *Three products from within fifty kilometres of
each other are copied all over the world and made properly only here. How a wheel of cheese, a leg of pork
and a barrel of cooked grape must are made, aged and judged.* (second sentence a fragment). Proposed:
**A wheel of cheese, a leg of pork and a barrel of cooked grape juice are all the same idea, a way of keeping
something through the winter, and each one is judged by a person with a tool in their hand.**

## Paragraphs

1. **Lead, a scene with a person and a tool.** A store in the hills between Parma and Modena, shelves of
   wheels to the roof, twelve months old. A man walks the rows with a small hammer, taps each wheel all over
   and listens. Hollows and cracks fail. What passes is branded with fire; what fails has its rind scored off
   and is sold as plain cheese. He is the battitore, and the three foods of this reading are all judged like
   that, by one person, by hand.
2. **Keeping through the winter** (label). The idea that joins them: milk, pork and grape juice all spoil,
   and each of these is a way of making them last. Protected designations mean the place, the ingredients and
   the method are fixed by law, and that the rest of the world copies and cannot match.
3. **Milk, fire and a hammer** (label). Mechanism as sequence: evening milk left overnight, cream skimmed,
   mixed with morning milk in a copper vat of 1,100 litres, calf rennet and yesterday's whey, curd broken to
   rice grains, cooked, lifted in a cloth, cut in two, two wheels a vat, brine three weeks, shelves twelve
   months at least. Ages after: 24 months the crystals people expect, 36 drier, 60 for enthusiasts. The
   monks of the Po abbeys in the twelfth century, Boccaccio's mountain of grated cheese in 1348, and the
   bank that keeps wheels in its vault as collateral: one sentence each, at most.
4. **Salt and the wind** (label). Heavy pigs on whey from the cheese, 160 kilos, hind leg only; Langhirano;
   sea salt, cold rooms, washing, halls with tall windows opened when the wind comes down the Val Parma; the
   sugnatura of lard and salt; fourteen months, often two years; the inspector with a needle of horse bone at
   five points, smelling. Then culatello on the foggy flats at Zibello, Verdi's country: the heart of the leg,
   sewn into a bladder, aged by fog; three times the price.
5. **A barrel in the attic** (label). Must of Trebbiano and Lambrusco boiled in an open pan to a syrup; the
   batteria of five or more barrels of different woods in the attic, summer heat and winter cold; the
   smallest barrel gives up a little each year and is topped from the next; nothing added, ever; twelve years,
   a panel of tasters, the 100 ml bottle; used by the drop. The person: the family that keeps a batteria and
   gives one to a daughter when she marries. Then the supermarket bottle: a different product.
6. **Ending, image.** The hammer, the needle and the ladle, or the daughter's barrels. No summary.
