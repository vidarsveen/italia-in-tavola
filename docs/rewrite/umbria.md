Map facts used without a separate source (general geography): Umbria between Tuscany, Lazio and the Marche; the Tiber
flowing south through it to Rome; Perugia on its hill above the Tiber valley; Assisi on the slope of Monte Subasio;
Montefalco, Bevagna, Trevi and Spello around the Spoleto valley; Orvieto on its rock above the Paglia in the south-west;
Norcia in the south-east under the Sibillini; Gubbio in the north-east; Torgiano south-east of Perugia where the Chiascio
joins the Tiber; Trasimeno west of Perugia near the Tuscan border; the Porziuncola a small church below Assisi; Preci and the
Val Castoriana near Norcia. General history: the Baglioni the family that dominated Renaissance Perugia; Saint Anthony Abbot
the patron of domestic animals and pigs; Dante exiled from Florence. General wine and food science: botrytis shrivels berries
and concentrates sugar; tannin binds to protein and fat, so meat softens it; a truffle's aroma dissolves in fat; vines were
once trained up trees in mixed fields ("married").
